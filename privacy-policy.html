Privacy Policy for BORED

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Aneman Systems built the BORED game as a Free game for users of all ages, including children. We take children's privacy very seriously.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

BORED does NOT collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information, including from children. We do NOT collect names, emails, photos, voice, precise location, or contacts.

2. Third-Party Services

BORED may use only Families-approved services:

  • Google Play Services (for app functionality)
  • Google AdMob for Families - non-personalized, child-appropriate ads only

These services may collect anonymous, non-personal information such as device type and app performance data to make the game work.

3. Advertising for Children

If ads are shown, they are non-personalized, age-appropriate, and compliant with Google Play Families Policy. No interest-based targeting or tracking for children.

4. Children's Privacy & Parental Rights

We comply with COPPA and Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
